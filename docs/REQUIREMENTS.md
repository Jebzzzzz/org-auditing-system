## Product boundary

OrgAudit is a production, VSU-only, multi-organization financial-accountability system. It preserves organization financial records across officer turnover.

In scope:

- VSU faculty, department, and organization administration.
- Position-based officer access, identity history, and controlled turnover.
- Semester opening balances, income and expense ledger, cash-in-bank and cash-on-hand balances, and internal transfers.
- Events/projects, categories, voucher numbering, revisions, voiding, audit history, dashboards, CSV export, and historical read-only records.
- Generation, versioning, signing, and download of **Attachment C - Organization Expenses**.

Out of scope:

- Attachment A (collections and income from students) source data and Attachment B (remaining collectibles) source data.
- Student collection/accounts-receivable workflows.
- Receipt uploads; PDFs generated by OrgAudit are the only planned object-storage use.
- A general university financial-report engine. A later, separate document-layout workflow may place A/B exports into Word templates.

## Actors

| Actor | Definition |
|---|---|
| Super-Admin | The system developer in charge; platform-level administrator, not an organization office. |
| Treasurer | Organization position account; financial writer. |
| Auditor | Organization position account; financial writer with the same source-data permissions as Treasurer. |
| President | Organization position account; financial-data reader, report generator/exporter, and signer. |

The authentication account belongs to the **position**, while `profiles` represent people. A person may have historical positions but may not hold more than one active officer position at the same time. One organization has one active holder per role.

## Institutional and tenure rules

- OrgAudit is exclusive to Visayas State University.
- Faculties and departments are fixed, seeded reference catalogs. An organization belongs to a department; its faculty is derived through the department.
- Only Super-Admin creates, activates, deactivates, or structurally changes organizations and initial position accounts.
- Officers can initiate turnover. Super-Admin reviews and completes it.
- On completion, the former holder is closed, the new holder becomes active, active sessions for the position account are invalidated, and the new officer must change the position-account password. The action is auditable.
- There are only First Semester and Second Semester reporting periods. Their calendar dates are optional metadata, not rigid financial validation rules.

## Accounting requirements

### Periods and opening balances

- A semester opening balance is entered manually for every organization and semester; it is never automatically carried forward.
- It stores separate Bank and Cash-on-Hand amounts. The previous closing amounts may be displayed as a non-binding comparison.
- An opening-balance creation or correction records the actor, timestamp, and a mandatory reason when changed.
- A period state is `open`, `closed`, or `archived`. Financial source data are writable only in an open period. Closed and archived periods remain viewable/exportable and are not physically deleted solely for storage performance.

### Transactions

- A transaction belongs to exactly one organization and academic period, and is either `income` or `expense`.
- It has exactly one fund source: `bank` or `cash_on_hand`.
- It may link to one category and one event; `event_id` is optional. Event-linked transactions may occur outside an event's dates.
- Required reporting fields are transaction date, reference, item details, amount, type, fund source, and voucher number. `reference` is the expenditure name/purpose printed in Attachment C; it is not the voucher number. `item_details` is free text because valid values include `5pcs`, `1 order`, and `prize`.
- Numeric quantity and unit price are optional. If both are supplied, the database must reject an amount that differs from quantity × unit price. Amounts and supplied numeric quantities must be positive.
- Every transaction gets an atomic voucher sequence scoped to organization and academic year. The displayed format is `YYYY-YYYY-0001`; numbering resets for each organization at each new academic year.
- Transactions are never hard-deleted. A transaction may be edited only with a required reason and immutable before/after revisions. It may be voided only with a required reason; a voided transaction remains visible and excluded from current balances and Attachment C.

### Transfers and balances

- A Bank-to-Cash-on-Hand or Cash-on-Hand-to-Bank movement is a first-class `fund_transfer`, not income or expense.
- Transfer source and destination must differ; amount is positive. It changes the two fund balances but never total liquidity.
- For an organization-period, Bank and Cash-on-Hand closing balances are calculated from opening balances, posted transactions by fund source, and posted transfers. They are not independently editable closing-balance fields.

## Events, categories, and Attachment C

- Events/projects represent report sections such as CS WEEK. An event has a title and occurrence dates; `start_date` is the Attachment C summary date for multi-day events.
- Categories classify spending analytically (for example Food, Equipment, Prizes) and are distinct from events and report references.
- Attachment C includes only posted **expense** transactions for one organization and semester.
- An expense linked to an event is grouped in that event's section. An expense without an event is grouped under the generated label **Miscellaneous**; Miscellaneous is not a fake event row.
- The report first renders `Date | Summary | Expenses`, then each section's `Date | Reference | Item/s | Amount` rows, subtotal, and grand total. The summary date is the event occurrence date (`events.start_date`), not a transaction date. Miscellaneous has no invented event date; its presentation date must be blank unless the approved template later requires another rule.
- Header, footer, institutional branding, document code, and pagination are presentation-template configuration, not financial facts. `ATTACHMENT-C.pdf` is the layout reference for content and grouping; its header/footer are explicitly subject to change.

## Report lifecycle

- The implementation term is `attachment_c_report`; it is not a live financial query once generated.
- A report contains immutable snapshot versions. Each version stores the source snapshot, rendered PDF location, hash, generator, and timestamp.
- Treasurer, Auditor, and President may sign a report version in any order, once each, using the active holder of their corresponding position. A signature is an approval timestamp tied to that version.
- Financial changes do not alter an existing signed version. A later generated version is distinct and requires new signatures. Earlier versions remain downloadable historical records.
- President may view, generate, export, and sign reports, but may not change financial source data.

## Security and quality requirements

- PostgreSQL/Supabase is the authoritative datastore; every organization-owned record is tenant-scoped at database level.
- Supabase RLS is the final access boundary. Client-side checks are only user-experience controls.
- UUIDs are used for IDs. Server code must treat IDs as strings and validate UUIDs; it must not convert them to numbers.
- All financial and security-sensitive mutations must identify the active actor, enforce organization/period scope, and produce an audit record.
- Secrets, service-role keys, and account credentials must never reach browser code or logs.
- Generated report PDFs and CSV exports must respect the same organization scope as on-screen data.

## Acceptance-level constraints

1. No organization user can read, create, modify, export, or sign another organization's data.
2. President can never create, revise, void, transfer, or modify opening balances.
3. A correction preserves both the original transaction and a reasoned revision trail.
4. A transfer does not change total liquidity or income/expense totals.
5. Attachment C uses the approved event/Miscellaneous grouping and event start date rule.
6. A signed report snapshot stays unchanged after later ledger changes.
